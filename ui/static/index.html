How to calibrate range: Assign leader/follower on the Ports tab first. Click Start Recording, then move every joint on both arms through its full range of motion — all the way to each mechanical stop. When done, click Stop & Save. Only range_min and range_max are updated; homing offsets and IDs are untouched.
